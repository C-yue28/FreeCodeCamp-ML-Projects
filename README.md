<h1>Portfolio of Machine Learning Projects from the freeCodeCamp developer course in ML</h1>

<h2>SMS Text Classifer</h2>
<p>Classifies an SMS text into spam/non-spam</p>
<p>100% accuracy on training data; ~98% accuracy on validation data; 100% accuracy on small sample of test data</p>

<h2>Book Recommendation Engine</h2>
<p>Finds 5 most related books to a given search using KNN algorithm</p>
<p>Achieved <3% loss with 100% accuracy on test examples</p>

<h2>Health Cost Prediction Engine</h2>
<p>Uses regression to predict health costs of individuals based on factors such as BMI, age, sex, location, etc.</p>
<p><$2200 MAE on test data</p>

<h2>Cat and Dog Image Classifier</h2>
<p>~65% accuracy on unseen images with a comparatively small training data size of 2000 images and only 6 epochs</p>

<h2>Rock Paper Scissors Bot</h2>
<p>Learns from the opponent's patterns; tracks the 4 most recent moves and maps to a predicted move</p>
<p>~70% winrate against other algorithms</p>
